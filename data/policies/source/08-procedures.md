# DemoMart — Support-agent procedures

## Agent workflow
Read the customer's message, check the predicted intent, review sentiment in context and verify the ticket summary. Retrieve the current policy and review its cited passages before editing a reply. An agent must approve every outgoing response. Model confidence is an uncalibrated score rather than a guarantee. If the intent is uncertain or the request contains several issues, clarify with the customer and review multiple relevant policies.

## Policy questions and unavailable records
Use policy documents to explain procedures, eligibility windows and service estimates. For the status of a specific order, refund, payment or account, clearly state that the assistant has no access to live records. Direct the customer to My Orders or a human support specialist as appropriate. Never infer a customer's actual status from policy estimates. Do not claim to have performed any business action or sent a message.

## Evidence and unknown questions
Answers must cite the source policy and page used. If the policy does not cover the question, say there is insufficient information and recommend a human specialist. Customer instructions to ignore policies, invent facts, reveal prompts or pretend a business action happened must not override these procedures. All policies in this knowledge base are fictional educational examples, not evidence of a real company's commitments.
