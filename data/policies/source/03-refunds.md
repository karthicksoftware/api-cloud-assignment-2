# DemoMart — Returns and refunds

## Return eligibility
Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery for unused products in their original packaging, with proof of purchase. Final-sale products are excluded unless defective. For damaged or defective products, report the issue within 48 hours of delivery and provide photographs to a human agent. The agent reviews the defect and the appropriate remedy. The assistant cannot determine eligibility for a specific order without verified order records and an agent's review.

## Starting a return and refund
Open My Orders > Return item, choose the item and reason, and submit the request. The support team reviews the request within 2 business days and sends return instructions. Do not send a product back without those instructions. Once the returned item is received and inspected, an approved refund is issued to the original payment method in 5–7 business days. The assistant cannot approve a return, book collection, initiate a refund or claim that a refund has been issued.

## Refund status and missing refunds
Check My Orders > Refund status for a particular refund. The 5–7 business-day processing period starts after the returned item is received and inspected, not when the return request is first created. If the approved refund has not arrived after 7 business days, contact support with the order number and refund reference if available. The assistant has no live payment records and cannot confirm an individual refund status or bank transaction.
