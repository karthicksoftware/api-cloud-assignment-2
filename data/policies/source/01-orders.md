# DemoMart — Orders and cancellations

## Placing and changing orders
DemoMart is a fictional Indian online retailer used only for this educational demonstration. All policies are synthetic and effective 1 November 2026. Prices are shown in INR. Customers place orders through the DemoMart website, selecting products, a delivery address and a supported payment method. Review the final amount and address before confirming. A confirmation email contains the order number. The support assistant cannot place an order or access customer records.

An order's items, quantities and shipping address can be changed only before dispatch. Customers should open My Orders, select the order and choose Edit order. If Edit order is unavailable, contact a human agent. After dispatch, the order cannot be edited; the customer can use the returns process after delivery. Changes may affect the order total. A human agent must confirm any change; an assistant's draft does not change an order.

## Cancellation window and fees
Customers may cancel an order before dispatch using My Orders > Cancel order. There is no cancellation fee before dispatch. Once an order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled; the customer may request a return after delivery subject to the returns policy. Do not promise to stop a dispatched parcel. Approved prepaid cancellations are refunded to the original payment method in 5–7 business days. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays. No cancellation, payment or refund can be executed by the assistant.

## Shipping addresses
To save a new shipping address, open Account > Addresses > Add address, enter the address and postal code, and select Save. Saving an address does not update an existing order. For an existing order, use My Orders > Edit order before dispatch. Support cannot promise an address change after dispatch. Agents must verify the customer through the approved customer-service process before handling account or order changes; the prototype has no verification service.
