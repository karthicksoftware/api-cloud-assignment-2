# DemoMart — Complaints and human support

## Customer-service channels
DemoMart support can be contacted at support@demomart.example or through Help > Contact support on the fictional website. The .example address is illustrative and does not receive email. Human agents are available Monday to Saturday, 09:00–18:00 IST, excluding public holidays. The target initial response time is one business day. The prototype cannot send an email, open a real ticket or connect to a live agent.

## Complaints and escalation
For a complaint, acknowledge the issue, summarize the facts provided and ask a human agent to review the relevant order or payment record. Record the order number only in the authenticated support system, not in a public demonstration. Escalate unresolved delivery, refund, account-access or payment problems to the appropriate specialist. Negative sentiment is a review signal, not proof of urgency or entitlement. Never promise compensation or guaranteed resolution without an approved policy and human authorization.

## Asking for a human agent
Customers may ask for a human at any point using Help > Contact support or the support email. Explain the available channels and operating hours. Outside operating hours, the customer can leave a message in the support system and expect an initial response within one business day. The assistant may recommend a handoff but cannot perform one. Immediate account-security concerns should be raised through the authenticated account-support flow; do not request secrets in chat.
