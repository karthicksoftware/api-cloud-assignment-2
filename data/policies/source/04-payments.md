# DemoMart — Payments and invoices

## Accepted payments
DemoMart accepts Visa and Mastercard debit or credit cards and UPI. Cash on delivery, cryptocurrency and instalment plans are not supported in this fictional demonstration. Supported payment options appear at checkout. Do not ask for a card number, CVV, PIN, password or one-time password in a support conversation. Customers enter payment details only in the secure checkout flow.

## Payment failures and duplicate charges
For a failed payment, confirm the payment method is supported, check the checkout error and try again only after confirming the order was not created. For an apparent duplicate charge or a debit without an order confirmation, contact a human support agent with the payment reference and approximate payment time. Never ask for full card details. An agent checks the payment system; the assistant cannot verify a charge, diagnose a bank failure, promise a reversal or request a second payment.

## Downloading and checking invoices
For dispatched orders, go to My Orders > View order > Download invoice. The invoice contains the order number, purchased items, tax amounts and total. If the invoice has a suspected error or is not available for a dispatched order, contact support with the order number and describe the discrepancy. The assistant can explain these steps but cannot retrieve an invoice or change its contents.
