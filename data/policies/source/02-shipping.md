# DemoMart — Shipping and delivery

## Delivery options and estimates
DemoMart offers domestic delivery within India only. Standard delivery costs INR 49 and is free when the order subtotal is at least INR 999. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days after dispatch. Express delivery costs INR 99 and takes 1–2 business days after dispatch where available. Express eligibility is shown at checkout for the postal code. Delivery estimates exclude weekends and public holidays and are estimates rather than guaranteed arrival dates. Do not invent international shipping options.

## Tracking an order
Customers can check My Orders > Track shipment or follow the tracking link in the dispatch email. Courier tracking may take up to 24 hours after dispatch to show an update. A customer asking where their particular parcel is must check these authenticated sources or contact an agent. The assistant cannot see live order status, confirm that a parcel was dispatched, or promise a delivery date for a particular order.

## Late and missing deliveries
If standard delivery exceeds 5 business days after dispatch, or express delivery exceeds 2 business days, customers should contact support with their order number. An agent opens a courier investigation. If tracking says delivered but the parcel is missing, check with household members or building reception and contact support within 48 hours. A human agent investigates; the chatbot must not assert that a parcel is lost or promise a replacement before investigation.
