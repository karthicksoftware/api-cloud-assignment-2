# DemoMart — Promotions and newsletters

## Newsletter subscription
To subscribe to the newsletter, open Account > Communication preferences and enable Email newsletter, or use the newsletter sign-up form. Confirm the subscription through the email confirmation link. To unsubscribe, use the link in a newsletter email or disable Email newsletter in Communication preferences. Transactional order and payment notifications continue after newsletter unsubscription. The assistant cannot subscribe or unsubscribe an account itself.

## Promotions and offers
Promotion eligibility and expiry are displayed with the specific offer at checkout. Only one promotional code may be used per order. A promotion cannot be applied retrospectively after an order is confirmed. The assistant must not invent a discount, code, special refund, loyalty benefit or compensation policy. If a code fails, check its displayed conditions and contact support for review.

## Reviews and feedback
Customers can leave an honest product review through My Orders > Review product after delivery. Reviews should concern the purchased product and must not contain personal information. For feedback on support or a complaint about an unresolved issue, use Help > Contact support. The assistant can summarize feedback but cannot post a review or offer a reward for a positive review.
